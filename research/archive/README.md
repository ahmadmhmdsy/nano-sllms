# research/archive — read-only record of refuted work

Purpose: keep the **mistakes** retrievable without letting them compete with
current truth. Nothing here is a recommendation; every entry is a dead end that
was actually run, with the lesson that came out of it.

## Rules

- **Read-only.** Do not extend or "fix" an archived item — open a new
  experiment instead.
- **Not current truth.** Current truth is `research/experiments.yaml` plus
  `research/verdict_audit.md`. This archive is history.
- **The originals stay where they are.** This is an INDEX, not a moved
  directory. The reports were left at their canonical paths because
  `research/EXPERIMENTS.md` (a frozen historical ledger by its own authority
  banner, lines 3–11) and the audit hub link to them; physically relocating them
  would break those links for no gain. What needed archiving was the *knowledge*,
  not the inode. The `origin` column names the exact path.
- **The wins are NOT archived.** Successes are curated in
  `research/WHAT_WORKS.md` (Proven levers) and `diacritizer/README.md`
  (Current best). This directory holds only what lost.

## Contents

- `diacritizer_failures.md` — every refuted D-line arm/track with its
  measured numbers, the reusable lesson, and the report path.
- Cross-line (core / Laya / mex) refuted levers are not duplicated here; they
  live in `research/WHAT_WORKS.md` §"What NOT to repeat" and `MEMORY.md`.
